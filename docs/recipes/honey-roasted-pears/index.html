The Method

4 STEPS / APPROX 45 MIN
  1. 01

    Heat oven to 190°C conventional / 170°C fan. Halve the pears and remove their cores.

  2. 02

    Mix melted butter, honey, lemon juice and cinnamon. Place pear halves cut-side up in a snug dish with the water.

  3. 03

    Spoon honey glaze over pears and roast for 25–35 minutes, basting once or twice, until tender and browned at the edges.

  4. 04

    Serve one warm pear half per person with thick cool yogurt, roasting syrup and roughly chopped toasted walnuts.

A NOTE FROM THE KITCHEN

Contains milk and walnuts. Hot sugar syrup can burn; honey is not suitable for infants under 12 months.
