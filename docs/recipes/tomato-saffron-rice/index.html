02 / LET'S COOK

The Method

5 STEPS / APPROX 45 MIN
  1. 01

    Soak saffron in 60 ml of the measured hot stock for 5 minutes. Heat the oven to 200°C conventional / 180°C fan if oven-roasting the tomatoes.

  2. 02

    Toss tomatoes with 15 ml of olive oil and roast around 20 minutes until softened, or blister in a skillet.

  3. 03

    Soften chopped onion in 20 ml olive oil, add garlic, then stir in the rinsed and drained rice for 1 minute.

  4. 04

    Add the measured stock, including the saffron infusion. Cover and cook over low heat about 12–15 minutes or as directed by rice packaging, until absorbed.

  5. 05

    Rest covered off the heat 5 minutes, fluff with a fork and top with tomatoes and fresh parsley.

A NOTE FROM THE KITCHEN

Some vegetable stocks contain allergens. Cool leftover cooked rice promptly; do not leave at room temperature.
