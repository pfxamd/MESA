02 / LET'S COOK

The Method

5 STEPS / APPROX 50 MIN
  1. 01

    Heat oven to 200°C conventional / 180°C fan. Halve aubergines lengthwise, score the flesh shallowly and season.

  2. 02

    Heat around 30 ml of olive oil in an ovenproof pan. Sear the aubergine halves cut-side down for 5–7 minutes until deeply browned.

  3. 03

    Move the pan to the oven, or transfer the aubergines to a tray, and roast for 20–25 minutes until fully tender.

  4. 04

    Mix thick yogurt with lemon, grated garlic, chopped parsley, remaining olive oil, salt and pepper.

  5. 05

    Spread the herbed yogurt over a plate, top with roasted aubergine and scatter toasted sesame over it.

A NOTE FROM THE KITCHEN

Contains milk and sesame. Use an ovenproof pan; the handle stays hot.
