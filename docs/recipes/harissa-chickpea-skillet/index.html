02 / LET'S COOK

The Method

5 STEPS / APPROX 30 MIN
  1. 01

    Heat oil in a deep skillet and soften the diced onion for 5 minutes.

  2. 02

    Add minced garlic and harissa; stir for 30–60 seconds until fragrant without burning.

  3. 03

    Add chopped tomatoes, drained chickpeas and water. Simmer gently for 10–12 minutes until the sauce thickens.

  4. 04

    Fold in spinach in batches until wilted, about 2 minutes. Taste before adding more salt; harissa varies in salt and heat.

  5. 05

    Serve from the skillet placed on a heat-safe trivet.

A NOTE FROM THE KITCHEN

Harissa heat and allergens vary by product. The skillet handle may be hot.
