The Good Pantry.

A little less, a little better.

FROM MARKET TO TABLETHE EVERYDAY TABLE
VOLUME 01 / STORY 03BACK TO ALL STORIES ↗

A useful pantry is not a museum of perfectly matching jars. It is a quiet collection of things that make an ordinary dinner possible.

Start with what you use

Dry pasta, rice, beans, chickpeas, good olive oil and a few seasonings can open more doors than an overflowing cupboard of forgotten ingredients. Choose what you genuinely cook, then replenish it as you go.

Decanting into clean jars can make quantities easy to see, but keeping the original packaging information nearby helps with dates, ingredients and allergens.

Everyday combinations

Beans become a lemony lunch. Rice welcomes a handful of herbs. Pasta can carry last night’s roasted peppers. A modest shelf is enough to support a lot of different meals.

Order without performance

Keep dry ingredients cool and dry, check dates and follow package storage instructions. The useful pantry is the one you can cook from, not the one that photographs best.

There is always something more to notice at the table.
