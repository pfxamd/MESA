## Locked photographic world

MESA is an independent contemporary culinary journal. Photograph edible, tactile food with ambitious art direction and accurate physical detail, in one coherent small editorial studio. Imagine a purposeful shoot assembled over one day: a weathered, matte limestone/sand-coloured tabletop; warm ivory paper; occasional sun-faded tomato-red linen; pale glazed ceramic plates; brushed stainless steel; restrained deep-green herbs; occasional lemon/citrus yellow. The tabletop and props recur *selectively* across photos, but each frame tells a different story.

Colours: tomato #E9472F, saffron #F6CA56, herb #32634A, paper #FFF7E8, ink #241D19. Treat these as palette guidance, **not blocks of uniform colour** or rules forcing every hue into every image. Natural colour, imperfect textures, subtle crumbs, minute condensation, authentic peel/wet pulp and believable cooking traces. Sophisticated editorial photography, *not* food delivery advertising.

Camera: photorealism, natural focal falloff, individual controlled lighting setups, 35–85mm full-frame equivalent where specified, clean authentic surfaces. Bright yet not blown-out. Genuine scale and materials. Hands only where directed, no identifiable faces. Compose at the exact aspect ratio; allow intentional breathing room for **HTML typography added later**, but do not paint words into photographs.

**Never include** any letters, legible text, symbols, logotypes, magazine headers, brand marks, labels, watermarks, graphic borders, websites, UI, mockups, frames, cheesy stock-food styling, levitating ingredients, physics-defying liquid, hyperreal plastic shine, artificial flames or unrelated decorative objects. Do not put recognizable branded packaging into frame.

**File contract:** Each image separately delivered in PNG at the closest supported high-quality native ratio. sRGB, opaque photograph, no simulated transparent checkerboard. Do not promise an unsupported exact pixel size. Filename is external metadata, not printed inside photo. Treat the six as six different shots. If a tool only creates one image per run, run it six independent times without concatenating them.

## Image 01 — The opening table

**Filename:** 01-opening-shared-table.png
**Aspect:** 16:9 wide horizontal. **Destination:** Home hero, primary opening image.
**Core idea:** A table that invites the viewer in, lively but considered — an ordinary lunch staged with artistic restraint, **not one of the 12 recipe hero dishes**.

**Composition:**
- Almost straight-down overhead shot, slightly off-centre, of a generous physical dining table under clear late-morning window light.
- Keep the **left 33–38%** calm and relatively light, with only subtle stone grain and soft table-shadow shapes. This area is intended to accommodate live browser headline text later; **do not write any headline into the photo**.
- On the centre-right: one broad irregular shallow white ceramic dish holding sliced ripe raw heirloom tomatoes with a little olive oil (NOT charred tomato toast), a small rough plate with torn bread, one small green-herb bowl and two halved lemons. Cropped edge of a rumpled red linen napkin at extreme right; no excessive clutter or perfect symmetry.
- Distinct layers and believable vessel size. One naturally used fork can sit near the tomato plate. Avoid a stock-style full breakfast arrangement.
- Focus overall sharp enough for a full-bleed desktop hero; hand-worked, slightly asymmetrical spacing. No hands and no people.

**Light & craft:** One large side window casting broad, soft directional shadows from top-right; tomato-red and citrus-yellow are the focal colours. 45–50mm normal-lens editorial realism. Polished but casually lived-in.
**Reject:** multi-panel food boards, top-down arrays of dozens of tiny ingredients, overlapping plates that violate physical space, baked dessert hero, recipe 07 plated toast, artificial oversaturated grocery-ad lighting.

## Image 02 — Tomato as material

**Filename:** 02-tomato-material-study.png
**Aspect:** 4:5 vertical. **Destination:** Home / material close-up and volume intro.
**Core idea:** The material beauty of the ingredient, not an assembled meal.

**Composition:**
- Close near-macro of **three** ripe heirloom tomatoes: one cross-section cut horizontally, one irregular wedge showing jelly-like seed chambers, one intact tomato set further back.
- Matte warm paper beneath with a little authentic tomato juice gathered naturally at the cut edge. Surface texture visibly tactile.
- Foreground cut tomato occupies bottom 55%; seed cavities and tiny skin folds in crisp focus, background fruit slightly soft. No plate, fork, basil garnish, liquid splash or kitchen utensils.
- Slightly diagonal arrangement with empty warm-paper space at top-left. Natural uneven fruit shapes rather than identical glossy spheres.

**Light & craft:** Soft diffused light from left, modest moisture specularity, red variation from coral to deep ripe crimson. Macro-equivalent 85mm short telephoto; true depth of field.
**Reject:** fake blood-like spills, impossible cellular geometry, surreal glowing tomatoes, typography/labels or recipe toast.

## Image 03 — A study in citrus and sunshine

**Filename:** 03-citrus-colour-study.png
**Aspect:** 4:5 vertical. **Destination:** Home editorial colour-break and link to S02.
**Core idea:** Graphic sunlight rendered with real photographic materials; an independent study distinct from 02.

**Composition:**
- Overhead still life of **one halved lemon**, **one halved orange**, **one whole lemon** and **one small detached leaf**, spaced confidently on warm yellow-toned paper.
- Three-quarter composition near lower-right; leave top-left calmer. Pulp cells visible and botanically believable. The halves sit naturally on their cut faces or tilted in physically possible positions — no levitation.
- Angular, long, hard-edged shadows from a small direct afternoon sun source cut diagonally across paper.

**Light & craft:** Hard sun, high contrast but visible highlights, deliberate geometric shadow rhythm; camera directly overhead, 50mm normal-equivalent.
**Reject:** sliced citrus arranged as a radial kaleidoscope, impossible pulp, confetti, lens flares, plastic 3D fruit, decorative text.

## Image 04 — Bread and crumb

**Filename:** 04-bread-and-crumb.png
**Aspect:** 3:2 horizontal. **Destination:** Visual chapter divider / bread editorial feature.
**Core idea:** A sensory moment of craft and touch, with no person needed.

**Composition:**
- A single handmade rustic sourdough loaf, **already torn by hand into two large rough pieces**, laid diagonally on slightly creased off-white butcher paper atop worn limestone.
- One part leans on the other in a believable way; open golden crumb pockets and rough brown crust with flour traces are the protagonists. A few naturally scattered bread crumbs trace away from the torn edge.
- Camera 35 degrees above table, focused on the torn interior. Keep modest uncluttered negative space toward right edge.
- A thin border of muted olive-green linen visible along far background edge, not a large decorative prop.

**Light & craft:** Slanting warm late-afternoon sun from side, strong tactile shadow and fine bread dust; 65–85mm portrait-lens feel.
**Reject:** full restaurant bread basket, butter bowl, unrealistic black holes within crumb, generic commercial bakery branding, toast topped with tomatoes, or process hands from image 05.

## Image 05 — Hands and herbs

**Filename:** 05-hands-and-herbs.png
**Aspect:** 3:2 horizontal. **Destination:** Kitchen introduction, human culinary gesture.
**Core idea:** A quiet, believable preparation moment rather than a polished plate.

**Composition:**
- Direct overhead action frame of **two anonymous adult hands** gently lifting a handful of rinsed **flat-leaf parsley** from a low brushed-steel colander or bowl; naturally separated leaflets and a few water droplets.
- Bowl sits slightly left of middle on matte sand-coloured surface. Light cotton sleeves in a neutral natural tone appear only at forearms. Keep wrists and fingers realistically joined and correctly counted.
- On the right a folded pale kitchen cloth is partially visible; a few detached parsley leaves rest naturally on surface.
- Avoid additional herb species that resemble basil/mint unless clearly incidental. No knife. No dish or cooked food. Gesture is calm and useful, not staged sprinkling confetti.

**Light & craft:** Soft side daylight with lifelike steel reflections, greens richly varied but natural, 50mm overhead, focus on hands and herb texture.
**Reject:** six fingers, fused stems and fingers, anthropomorphic leaves, half-visible faces, chef uniform, glamorised model portrait, fake particle effects.
